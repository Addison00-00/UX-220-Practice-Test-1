Emphasis And Strengthened Text

A paragraph is a self-contained unit of writing with one or more sentences that focus on a single main idea, theme, or topic, indicated by a new line, indentation, or numbering.

Paragraphs are usually associated with academic writing, as they are commonly used in assignments, essays, theses, research papers, and more. However, paragraphs can take different forms, such as creative writing for character dialogues and monologues in comics and novels, or stylized typography posters in graphic design. They also present information in infographics, instruction guides and manuals, news articles and journalism. Or in daily usage, such as emails or long text messages to friends and partners during breakups.
